# 🎥 smart-video-reframe - Effortlessly Reframe Videos for Social Media

## 🛠️ Overview
Smart Video Reframe is an application that helps you convert landscape videos into vertical format. This service is perfect for sharing on platforms like TikTok, Reels, and Shorts. It uses AI to detect faces and analyze scenes, ensuring that the most important parts of your video stay in focus when you transform its size.

Once installed, you can easily reframe your videos. Here’s how to do it:

1. Open Smart Video Reframe.

5. Once it's done, your new video will be saved in the selected directory.

## ⌨️ Command Line Interface (Optional)
For users comfortable with the command line, Smart Video Reframe offers a CLI tool that allows advanced users to reframe videos quickly and efficiently. Here’s a simple example:

```bash
python smart_video_reframe.py --input your_video.mp4 --output framed_video.mp4
```

This command will convert `your_video.mp4` to the vertical format and save it as `framed_video.mp4`.
